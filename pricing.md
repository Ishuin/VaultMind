# ThoughtWeb Navigator Pricing

ThoughtWeb Navigator offers flexible subscription plans designed to meet the needs of different users, from individuals to enterprises. Each plan provides access to our core features with varying levels of usage limits and premium capabilities.

## Subscription Plans

### Free Tier

**Price: $0/month**

Perfect for casual users who want to explore ThoughtWeb Navigator's basic capabilities.

**Features:**
- Access to basic knowledge management tools
- Up to 5 sources (files, websites, etc.)
- Standard LLM model access
- 50 queries per month
- 100MB storage limit
- Community support

### Personal Plan

**Price: $9.99/month** or **$99/year (save 17%)**

Ideal for individual users who need more capacity and advanced features.

**Features:**
- Everything in Free tier
- Up to 50 sources
- Access to standard and advanced LLM models
- 500 queries per month
- 1GB storage limit
- Priority email support
- Advanced search capabilities
- Custom knowledge organization
- Export functionality

### Professional Plan

**Price: $19.99/month** or **$199/year (save 17%)**

Designed for professionals who rely on knowledge management for their work.

**Features:**

- 2,000 queries per month
- 5GB storage limit

- Collaboration with up to 3 users

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, you can change your subscription plan at any time. When upgrading, you'll be charged the prorated difference for the remainder of your billing cycle. When downgrading, the new rate will apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

### Is there a free trial?
Yes, new users can try the Personal plan free for 14 days. No credit card required for the trial period.

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and PayPal.

### Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. If you're not satisfied with your subscription, contact our support team within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

### What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
If you approach your monthly limits, you'll receive notifications. Once you reach your limit, you'll need to upgrade to a higher tier or wait until your limits reset at the beginning of your next billing cycle.

### Do you offer discounts for educational institutions or non-profits?
Yes, we offer special pricing for educational institutions, non-profit organizations, and students. Please contact our sales team for more information.
